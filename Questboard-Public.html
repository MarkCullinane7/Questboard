Settings & data

Import a CSV to add or replace your plans. Your current progress is saved in this browser on this computer.

CSV columns: Plan, Category, Name, Points, Days, PlanDays, CompletionBonus, Date
Category: Daily, Bonus, or Task. Days: Everyday, Weekdays, Weekends, or individual days (e.g. Mon;Wed;Fri). PlanDays controls automatic plan selection. Date is optional (YYYY-MM-DD) for date-specific Tasks.

Import behavior: choose whether to replace all plans or add/update plans by name. Existing progress is kept where task IDs still match.

Plan selection: automatic selection uses the plan whose PlanDays matches today. You can manually switch plans before completing any task. Once a task is completed, the plan is locked for the rest of that day to protect your score.
